# Rome to Bar to North Adriatic Classic (14 Days)

## Overview & Cost Estimate

**Trip Duration:** 14 days | **Distance:** ~1,100+ km mixed transit and hiking  
**Best Season:** May–September (optimal: June–July)  
**Projected Budget (per person):** €2,400–3,500 (mid-range)

### Budget Breakdown
- **Accommodation:** €800–1,200 (3-star, ~€60–85/night)
- **Food & Dining:** €700–1,050 (mix of street food, local trattorias, restaurants; ~€50–75/day)
- **Transport (trains, car rental, ferries, buses):** €500–700
- **Activities & Park Fees:** €150–250
- **Contingency & Misc:** €250–300

---

## Focus
Long-form classic route starting in Rome, dropping to Bar, then running north with bigger mountain and park hiking blocks.

## Major Hiking Modules

### Day 5: Kotor Vrmac Viewpoint Walk
**Difficulty:** Moderate | **Duration:** 2–3 hours | **Distance:** 4–6 km
**Elevation Gain:** ~300–450 m
**Description:** Shorter, well-marked route to an excellent viewpoint overlooking Kotor Bay. Suitable for most travellers wanting great views without a full-day hike.
**Trailhead:** Kotor old town entrance

### Day 9: Biokovo Ridge View
**Difficulty:** Moderate | **Duration:** 2–3 hours | **Distance:** 4–6 km
**Elevation Gain:** ~300–500 m
**Description:** A shorter ridge walk delivering the key alpine viewpoints and coastal panoramas without a long summit push.
**Trailhead:** Makarska Riviera base

### Day 11: Paklenica Canyon Half-Day
**Difficulty:** Moderate | **Duration:** 2–3 hours | **Distance:** 6–8 km
**Elevation Gain:** ~300–500 m
**Description:** A recommended half-day route that showcases the canyon's main features and scenic passages without the full-day effort.
**Trailhead:** Paklenica National Park entrance

### Day 12: Plitvice Upper Ridge Trek
- **Difficulty:** Moderate-Hard | **Duration:** 7 hours | **Distance:** 16 km
- **Elevation Gain:** ~600 m
- **Description:** Upper lake boardwalk network combined with ridge traverse. Lower lake descent circuit provides scenic finish. BoardWalks are well-maintained.
- **Trailhead:** Plitvice Lakes National Park, upper entrance
- **Map:** Hiking leg shown on the route map (OpenStreetMap footpaths via OSRM).

## Activity Depth Options

**Heritage-Heavy Variant:**
- Extend Days 4, 7, 8, 13 with additional museum visits and old-town exploration.
- Budget +€150–200 for extra guides or museum entries.

**Nature-Heavy Variant:**
- Add half-day hikes on Days 10–12; more time at Krka and Plitvice.
- Budget +€100–150 for park extensions and local guide.

**Mixed-Pace (Recommended):**
- Keep one low-activity evening after each long-hike day for recovery.
- Rotates between culture, hiking, and relaxation.

---

## Logistics & Notes

- **Trains:** Book Rome→Naples and Naples→Bari on Trenitalia/Italo in advance; Termini and Centrale stations are walkable from city centres.
- **Driving:** Rent car in Bari; return in Trieste (or reverse). Budget ~€40–60/day.
- **Ferries:** Bari→Bar is scenic; book in advance (€30–50 per person).
- **Hiking Season:** Full hiking recommended May–Sept; shorter hikes at shoulder season.
- **Food Notes:** Fresh seafood abundant; local wines €12–20/bottle. Inland days offer hearty pasta and meat dishes.
- **Weather Risk:** Summer heat (July–Aug) can be intense; May/June and Sept are ideal.
