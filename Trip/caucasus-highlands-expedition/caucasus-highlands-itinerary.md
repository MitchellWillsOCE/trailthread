# Caucasus Highlands Expedition (10 Days)

## Overview & Cost Estimate

**Trip Duration:** 10 days | **Distance:** ~1,200 km mountain roads and alpine trekking  
**Best Season:** June–September (optimal: July–August)  
**Projected Budget (per person):** AUD 2,000–2,600 (mid-range, budget-friendly)

### Budget Breakdown
- **Accommodation:** AUD 400–600 (mix of guesthouses & 3-star; ~AUD 40–60/night)
- **Food & Dining:** AUD 500–700 (local markets, traditional cuisine; ~AUD 50–70/day)
- **Transport (car rental, local transfers):** AUD 350–500
- **Activities & Parks (guides, permits):** AUD 300–400
- **Contingency & Misc:** AUD 150–200

---

## Theme
Mountain roads, fortress heritage, and alpine hiking through Georgia and Armenia with dramatic Caucasus views.

## Major Hiking Modules

### Day 3: Gergeti Ridge Viewpoint Walk
- **Difficulty:** Easy-Moderate | **Duration:** 1.5–2 hours | **Distance:** ~2.5 km
- **Elevation Gain:** ~250–350 m
- **Description:** Shorter, well-marked ascent to a panoramic viewpoint near Gergeti Trinity Church. Designed to offer the iconic views with minimal exertion and time commitment.
- **Trailhead:** Kazbegi village base

### Day 6: Garni Gorge Photo Loop
- **Difficulty:** Easy-Moderate | **Duration:** 3 hours | **Distance:** 5 km
- **Elevation Gain:** ~200 m
- **Description:** Scenic gorge walk featuring dramatic rock formations. Geghard Monastery sits above. Low-traffic trail with excellent photography opportunities.

### Day 7: Azhdahak Crater Rim Walk (Short Option)
- **Difficulty:** Moderate | **Duration:** 2–3 hours | **Distance:** 4–6 km
- **Elevation Gain:** ~300–450 m
- **Description:** A shorter rim walk giving crater viewpoints and high alpine scenery without committing to a full-day summit push. Safer for acclimatisation and fits better in tight itineraries.
- **Note:** 4x4 transfer recommended for access; consider a guide for conditions.

## Activity & Pace Options

**Adventure-Heavy Variant:**
- Extend Day 7 with longer crater circuit.
- Add technical climbing optional module (Kazbek guides available).
- Budget +AUD 200–300.

**Culture-Heavy Variant:**
- Reduce hiking to Days 3 & 6 only.
- Extend monastery and archaeological site visits.
- Budget -AUD 100–150.

**Altitude-Friendly Pacing:**
- Keep full itinerary but add extra acclimatization day in Kazbegi.
- Take Day 4 as easier recovery.

## Regional Notes

- **Visas:** Australians get 365 days visa-free in Georgia; Armenia requires visa (obtainable on arrival or pre-arranged).
- **Currency:** Georgian Lari (GEL) ~AUD 0.40 per unit; Armenian Dram (AMD) ~AUD 0.002 per unit. Both very budget-friendly.
- **Best Time:** July–August for clear alpine weather; accept afternoon cloud cover.
- **Food & Drink:** Khachapuri AUD 4–7; traditional stews AUD 8–12; local wine AUD 3–8/bottle.
- **4x4 Guides:** Highly recommended for Days 3 & 7 mountain sections (~AUD 80–120/day).
- **Weather:** Mountain weather volatile; sunset starts essential. Temperatures can drop rapidly above 3,000 m.
