# Vietnam Heritage and Nature Route (11 Days)

## Overview & Cost Estimate

**Trip Duration:** 11 days | **Distance:** ~1,000 km north-south with cave/park modules  
**Best Season:** October–April (optimal: November–March)  
**Projected Budget (per person):** AUD 1,600–2,000 (budget-friendly)

### Budget Breakdown
- **Accommodation:** AUD 250–350 (mix 2-3 star, street-side budget; ~AUD 25–35/night)
- **Food & Dining:** AUD 350–500 (street food emphasis, local restaurants; ~AUD 35–45/day)
- **Transport (buses, some flights, car rentals):** AUD 350–500
- **Activities & Guides (cave guide, cruises, permits):** AUD 300–400
- **Contingency & Misc:** AUD 150–200

Urban culture, heritage sites, bay cruising, and cave/park adventure blocks through historic north-south corridor.

## Major Activity Modules

### Day 3: Ha Long Bay Cruise
- **Difficulty:** Easy | **Duration:** Full day | **Distance:** Maritime
- **Description:** UNESCO limestone karsts cruise with grottoes and island stops. Overnight option available. Sunset on water is quintessential North Vietnam experience.
- **Cost:** AUD 80–120 (includes meals, boat)

### Day 6: Marble Mountains Cave-Temple Circuit
**Difficulty:** Easy-Moderate | **Duration:** 2–3 hours | **Distance:** ~3–4 km
**Elevation Gain:** ~100–150 m
**Description:** Shorter circuit visiting the main caves and viewpoints; easy stairs and photo stops allow more time for beaches and markets in the afternoon.

### Day 8: Imperial City + Mausoleum Full-Day Tour
- **Difficulty:** Easy (walking) | **Duration:** 7–8 hours
- **Description:** Guided complex of ancient imperial citadel, mausoleums, and administrative buildings. Perfume River scenery included. Book guide morning-of for best rates (AUD 25–50).

### Day 10: Phong Nha Cave Walk
**Difficulty:** Easy-Moderate | **Duration:** 2–3 hours | **Distance:** 3–5 km
**Elevation Gain:** Minimal
**Description:** Guided cave walk covering accessible cave passages and riverside trails; designed to limit time underground while showcasing karst features. Suitable for most fitness levels.
**Cost:** Guide + cave entry AUD 50–80

## Activity & Pace Options

**Budget Backpacker Variant:**
- Skip Ha Long Bay (save AUD 80–120); substitute with day-trip to Haiphong.
- Use overnight buses between cities to save accommodation.
- Budget AUD 1,200–1,500.

**Nature-Heavy Variant:**
- Add 2-day Sapa trekking (north of Hanoi) for mountain hiking and minority villages.
- Extend Phong Nha time to 2–3 days with multiple caves.
- Budget +AUD 200–300.

**Culture-Immersion Variant:**
- Keep full itinerary with added cooking classes (AUD 25–40 each day).
- Extend Hue mausoleum visits with specialist guides.
- Budget +AUD 100–150.

## Regional Notes

- **Visas:** Australian citizens need visa; E-visa available online (AUD 30–40, 3-day processing).
- **Currency:** Vietnamese Dong (VND); ~AUD 1 = 16,700 VND. Cash is king; ATMs readily available.
- **Best Time:** November–March for mild weather; avoid June–September (heat/dengue).
- **Food & Drink:** Pho/banh mi AUD 1–3; restaurant meals AUD 5–12; beer AUD 1–2/bottle.
- **Transport:** Buses comfortable and cheap (AUD 15–30 overnight); trains available but slower.
- **Cave Guides:** Mandatory for Phong Nha; English-speaking guides AUD 50–80 for group.
- **Weather:** Humidity high year-round; rain likely Oct–April in north.
