## Major Hiking Modules

### Day 2: Mljet Salt Lake Circuit Hike
**Difficulty:** Moderate | **Duration:** 3 hours | **Distance:** ~6–8 km
**Elevation Gain:** ~150 m
**Description:** Shortened loop around the salt lakes focusing on key viewpoints and swim stops; leaves time for ferry connections and relaxed afternoon pacing.
**Trailhead:** Mljet NP entrance

### Day 5: Krka Waterfall Boardwalk
- **Difficulty:** Easy-Moderate | **Duration:** 4 hours | **Distance:** 6 km
- **Elevation Gain:** ~100 m
- **Description:** Well-maintained boardwalk network through national park. Multiple waterfall viewpoints. Swimming pools accessible. Minimal elevation change but can be slippery after rain.
- **Cost included:** Park entry (AUD 30–50)
- **Map:** Canyon hike leg snapped to OpenStreetMap trails on the route map.

### Day 6: Paklenica Canyon Short Route
**Difficulty:** Moderate | **Duration:** 2–3 hours | **Distance:** 6–8 km
**Elevation Gain:** ~250–400 m
**Description:** Recommended half-day canyon loop to enjoy dramatic limestone sections without a full-day commitment. Start early for cooler conditions.

### Day 7: Plitvice Highlights Loop
**Difficulty:** Moderate | **Duration:** 3–4 hours | **Distance:** 6–8 km
**Elevation Gain:** ~200 m
**Description:** Shortened highlights loop combining upper and lower viewpoints with boat shuttle options to reduce on-foot time while still seeing the signature cascades.
**Cost included:** Park entry (AUD 45–70)

## Activity & Pace Options

**Nature-Heavy Variant:**
- Extend Days 2, 5, 6, 7 with additional park exploration.
- Consider bike rental in Split area (Day 4).
- Budget +AUD 120–180.

**Relaxed Coastal Pacing:**
- Skip one full-day hike; keep Days 4 & 8 as recovery.
- Emphasize ferry experiences and island time.
- Budget -AUD 60–100.

**Mixed Active-Culture:**
- Keep all hikes but reduce durations (shorter trail variants available).
- Add guide for Split Roman history (AUD 50–80).

## Regional Notes

- **Visas:** Australian citizens get 90 days visa-free in Croatia.
- **Currency:** Euro (EUR); ~AUD 1.65 per EUR.
- **Ferries:** Book in advance; add 20–30 mins boarding buffer.
- **Park Fees:** Krka AUD 30–50; Plitvice AUD 45–70; Paklenica AUD 25–40.
- **Best Time:** May–June for mild temps; early Sept for fewer tourists.
- **Food & Drink:** Fresh seafood AUD 15–25; local wines AUD 12–25/bottle.
- **Swimming:** Bring towel; refreshing in all coastal and lake areas.
- **Weather:** Summer heat can exceed 30°C; parks offer shade in forest sections.
