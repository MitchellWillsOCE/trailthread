## Major Activity & Heritage Modules

### Day 7: Alhambra Fortress Complex
- **Difficulty:** Easy (walking) | **Duration:** 6–7 hours
- **Description:** UNESCO palace-fortress with intricate Moorish architecture, fountains, gardens. Book timed entry (AUD 25–40) to skip queues. Arabic geometric patterns and historical depth unparalleled.
- **Timing:** Early entry (8 AM) recommended.

### Day 8: Albaicin Historic Quarter Loop
- **Difficulty:** Easy | **Duration:** 3 hours | **Distance:** 4 km
- **Elevation Gain:** ~80 m
- **Description:** Narrow cobbled streets, whitewashed buildings, viewpoints overlooking Alhambra. Local artisan quarter with cafes. Medieval atmosphere preserved. Can feel touristy; explore morning or evening.

### Day 10: Rock of Gibraltar Trail Hike
**Difficulty:** Easy-Moderate | **Duration:** 2–3 hours | **Distance:** 5 km
**Description:** Mediterranean vistas, Barbary macaque sightings, wind-carved limestone formations. Cable car option available for descent (AUD 15–20). Park entry required (AUD 15–25).

## Regional Notes

- **Visas:** Australian citizens get 90 days visa-free in Schengen zone (Spain included).
- **Currency:** Euro (EUR); ~AUD 1.65 per EUR.
- **Car Rental:** Recommended for flexibility; ~AUD 35–50/day. Toll roads in Spain ~AUD 20–50 on major routes.
- **Best Time:** May–June for mild temps; Sept–Oct for fewer tourists. July–Aug can exceed 40°C in south.
- **Food & Drink:** Tapas AUD 4–8 each; restaurant meals AUD 15–30; wine AUD 15–25/bottle.
- **Museums:** Alhambra AUD 25–40; Prado AUD 20–30; many free evening hours (check ahead).
- **Gibraltar Specifics:** British territory; passport required. Pound Sterling used alongside Euro. Rock entry AUD 15–25.
- **Border Timing:** Spain-Gibraltar border can have queues; budget 30–60 mins extra for Day 9 transfer.
