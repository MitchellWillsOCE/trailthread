## Hiking & Activity Highlights

### Day 3: Kotor Fortress Hike
- **Difficulty:** Moderate | **Duration:** 4 hours | **Distance:** 9 km
- **Elevation Gain:** ~400 m
- **Description:** Scenic climb through Mediterranean forest to fortification ruins. Views over Kotor Bay and surrounding mountains. Well-marked trail with photo stops.
- **Trailhead:** Kotor old town entrance
- **Map:** Fortress hike route on the journey map (OSM footpaths).

### Day 4: Blue Cave & Boat Experience
- **Difficulty:** Easy | **Duration:** 2–3 hours
- **Description:** Boat tour from Herceg Novi to famous sea cave. Swimming opportunity. Kanli kula fortress walk combines cultural and scenic elements.

### Day 8: Badanj Cave Heritage Walk
- **Difficulty:** Easy | **Duration:** 3 hours (guided)
- **Description:** Archaeological site with Paleolithic rock art. Local historian guide provides cultural context. Easy walking on cave grounds.
- **Cost:** AUD 100–150 (includes guide)

## Pace & Logistics Options

**Culture-Heavy Variant:**
- Extend Days 1, 2, 7 with museum visits and guided tours.
- Budget +AUD 80–150 for extra guide services and entries.

**Active Variant:**
- Add kayaking on Day 4 or 6 (~AUD 60–100).
- Include optional easier hikes on Days 5–6.

**Relaxed Pacing (Recommended):**
- Keep Days 6 and 9 as buffer/rest days.
- No hikes over 5 km except Kotor fortress.

## Regional Notes

- **Visas:** Australian citizens get 90 days visa-free in Montenegro.
- **Currency:** Euro (EUR); ~AUD 1.65 per EUR.
- **Best Time:** May & June for mild temps; Sept for fewer tourists.
- **Food & Drink:** Fresh seafood €8–15; local wines AUD 15–30/bottle.
- **Transport:** Car rental ~AUD 35–50/day; buses available but car gives flexibility.
- **Weather:** Summer heat can exceed 30°C; coastal breeze mitigates.
