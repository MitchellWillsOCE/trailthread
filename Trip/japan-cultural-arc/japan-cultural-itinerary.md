## Major Cultural & Heritage Modules

### Day 3: Kenroku-en Garden + Samurai District
- **Difficulty:** Easy | **Duration:** 3 hours | **Distance:** 4 km
- **Description:** Japan's largest traditional garden with seasonal beauty (cherry blossoms spring, autumn colors fall). Adjacent samurai district has historical residences and tea houses. Peak season can be crowded; early start recommended.
- **Garden Entry:** AUD 10–15
- **Best Light:** Early morning (7–8 AM) for photographic quality

### Day 6: Osaka Castle & Street Food Route
- **Difficulty:** Easy | **Duration:** 2+ hours | **Distance:** 3 km
- **Description:** Reconstructed 16th-century castle with surrounding park and moats. Street food quarter (Dotonbori) has takoyaki, okonomiyaki, ramen stalls. Modern castle interior + historic exterior blend old/new Japan.
- **Castle Entry:** AUD 12–18
- **Food Budget:** AUD 20–40 for full street food exploration

### Day 7: Nara Day-Trip Option
- **Difficulty:** Easy | **Duration:** Full day (can fit in 5–6 hours)
- **Description:** Sacred deer roam freely around temples. UNESCO sites include Todai-ji Temple (world's largest wooden building) and Nara Park. 45 mins by train from Osaka.
- **Cost:** Train + entry AUD 40–60
- **Shinto & Buddhist Heritage:** Syncretic temple architecture unparalleled.

## Regional Notes

- **Visas:** Australian citizens get 90 days visa-free in Japan.
- **Currency:** Japanese Yen (JPY); ~AUD 1 = 90 JPY. Cash essential; credit cards not universal outside major cities.
- **Rail Pass:** JR Pass ~AUD 300–400 for 7 days; excellent for Shinkansen access.
- **Best Time:** Cherry blossom season (late March–early April) and autumn foliage (October–November) are peak but crowded. May, June, Sept have good weather with fewer tourists.
- **Food & Drink:** Ramen AUD 7–12; sushi AUD 8–30/plate; beer AUD 5–8/bottle; sake AUD 4–10/glass.
- **ATMs:** 7-Eleven convenience store ATMs accept foreign cards reliably.
- **Transportation:** IC cards (Suica/Pasmo; AUD 15 deposit) work on trains, buses, buses across regions.
- **Scheduling:** Book gardens and popular attractions early; timed entry systems common.
- **Language:** English signage in major cities; translation apps helpful for menus and directions.
