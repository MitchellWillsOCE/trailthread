    Welcome

    Plan routes. Explore places.

    Trailthread is a travel map for browsing multi-day routes, reading day-by-day stories, and discovering cities, national parks, and hiking trails—similar to a digital trip journal on a map.

    1. Explore Turn on city, park, trail, and landmark layers in Map options—no journey required.
    2. Pick a journey Open a sample route to see the path, stops, and full itinerary.
    3. Read & navigate Use Story and Stops tabs once a journey is loaded.
